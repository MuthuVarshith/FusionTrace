How It Works

Our advanced detection process is simple, secure, and delivers results in seconds.

01

Upload Media

Upload one image, audio file, or video for analysis. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP, WAV, MP3, M4A, MP4, MOV, and WebM.

02

Secure Transfer & Preprocessing

The system validates the file, inspects technical properties, normalizes audio, and extracts sampled video frames when applicable.

03

Select Detection Model

The system automatically selects the optimal AI model based on media type or lets users choose manually for specialized detection.

04

Run Deepfake Detection

AI models analyze the media for signs of manipulation, synthetic patterns, and inconsistencies across visual or audio signals.

05

Get Instant Results

Receive a weighted risk assessment and the detector evidence used to produce it. It is not proof of authenticity or manipulation.

06

Ensure Privacy & Security

Self-hosted deployments control media retention. This installation defaults to a 24-hour retention setting; automatic cleanup must be enabled by the operator.
